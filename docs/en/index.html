About Yarncue,
in a few answers.

What can I manage with Yarncue?

Projects, yarn, patterns, progress, and notes can all live together in one place.

Is Yarncue good for beginners?

Yes. Start with one project and keep only the details you need, at your own pace.

Does it work for knitting and crochet?

Yes. Record the yarn, needles or hook, rows, pattern, and notes for either kind of project.
